# The risk engine, in numbers

Generated by `npm run engine:readme` from `src/lib/data/families.ts` and `src/lib/engine/risk.ts`. Every number below is the one the code uses.

## Pipeline for one fragment

1. **Retrieve.** Moss `queryMultiIndex` over the playbook (+ community intel), `alpha = 1.0`, top-6 → raw cosine scores.
2. **Calibrate.** `confidence = clamp((score − floor) / (ceil − floor), 0, 1)` with floor 0.38, ceil 0.72 (moss-minilm).
3. **Gate.** Fragments under 4 words are ignored. Caller fragments containing a *benign marker* (see below) are ignored. If the best benign look-alike scores within 0.05 of the best tactic line, the fragment is ignored.
4. **Credit.** Tactic lines within 0.06 of the best tactic line (max 3) are credited with rank discounts 1.0 / 0.75 / 0.55; anything below confidence 0.3 is dropped. The protected person's words can only credit `victim_compliance` lines; the caller's words never can.
5. **Accumulate.** Per tactic, keep the best `confidence × (0.6 + 0.4 × severity/5)` seen in the call.
6. **Score.** `score = 100 × (1 − Π_t (1 − weight_t × 0.32 × conf_t))`, then rules: pressure + ask (each ≥ 0.45) ⇒ ≥ 75; compliance under pressure ⇒ ≥ 85; ≥ 4 distinct tactics across ≥ 2 fragments ⇒ ≥ 60; 3 distinct ⇒ ≥ 35.
7. **Level.** safe < 25 ≤ caution < 60 ≤ danger.

"Alone" is the score a single tactic can reach by itself: at most 32, barely into CAUTION and far from DANGER (60); the score comes from *combinations*, which is what a scam script is.

## Benign markers (regular expressions)

- `\b(will|would|do|does|don'?t|won'?t|shall)\s+(never|not)\s+(ask|need|require|request)\b`
- `\bnever\s+(share|give|tell|read out)\b.*\b(otp|pin|cvv|password|code)`
- `\bdo not share\b.*\b(otp|pin|cvv|password)`
- `\b(no|nothing)\s+(payment|fee|charge|advance)\b.*\b(required|needed|to be made|in advance)`
- `\b(you\s+)?(don'?t|do not|won'?t) (need|have) to (do|share|pay|send) anything\b`
- `\bcall (us|back|the number)\b.*\b(on|from|at) (the )?(back of|official|card|website|app|number on)`
- `\bhang up and call\b`
- `\b(at your convenience|whenever (is )?convenient|no rush|no hurry)\b`
- `\bthrough the (official|app|website|portal|counter)\b`
- `\bpay(able)? at the (counter|reception|desk|branch|store)\b`
- `\bwe (will|'ll) (send|post|email|mail) (you )?(a|the) (letter|notice|copy)\b`

## Worked example

Authority (0.8) at confidence 0.8 → 1 − (1 − 0.8·0.32·0.8) = 0.205 → score 20 (safe). Add urgency (0.8 @ 0.8): score 37 (caution). Add an OTP request (1.0 @ 0.8): triad ⇒ 75 (danger).
